# Acme Corp Access Control Policy

Version 2.0
Effective Date: 03 March 2024

## 1. Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is required for all employee accounts, including
standard user accounts, without exception for routine access.

## 2. Exceptions

Break-glass service accounts are exempt from multi-factor authentication and
are instead protected by hardware tokens held in a sealed safe. Every use of a
break-glass account raises an alert to the security team within five minutes.

## 3. Password Standard

Passwords must be at least 16 characters and are rotated every 180 days.
